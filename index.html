BlockPulse Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 12, 2026

This policy applies to the BlockPulse Android game and related services provided by Serhat Yokuşoğlu and published under MegaSpirits (“we,” “us,” or “Service Provider”). Contact us at syokusoglu1@gmail.com.

Guest play

You can play without signing in. Guest progress and preferences are stored locally on your device. Clearing BlockPulse app storage or uninstalling the app removes local data.

Google sign-in and cloud game data

Google sign-in is optional. If you choose it, Google and Firebase Authentication process your Firebase user ID, Google account email, display name, and, if available, profile photo URL to authenticate you and sync your progress. BlockPulse does not receive or store your Google password.

Firebase Cloud Firestore stores your cloud profile and gameplay data, such as level progress, scores, stars, in-game currency, streak information, and record update times. Your display name, score, level reached, and stars submitted to the leaderboard can be visible to other signed-in BlockPulse players.

AI hints

When you choose the AI Hint feature, the current puzzle board, available piece types, and current and target scores are sent through Firebase AI Logic to Google's Gemini service to generate a hint. The hint request is optional; you can keep playing without using it. Google processes the request under its applicable service terms and privacy disclosures.

Advertising and privacy choices

BlockPulse displays banner, interstitial, and rewarded ads through Google Mobile Ads (AdMob). Google and its advertising service providers may process device or advertising identifiers, IP address and approximate location, app interactions, and diagnostic information to deliver, measure, and protect ads. Data and ad personalization depend on your region, device settings, ad configuration, and consent choices.

Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) presents privacy messages and manages advertising choices where required. The app checks whether ads may be requested before requesting them. You can open this policy from Settings and use the Privacy options entry in Settings when it is available.

Learn more: Google Privacy Policy · How Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Third-party services and sharing

The app uses Google Play Services, Firebase Authentication, Firebase Cloud Firestore, Firebase AI Logic/Gemini, Google Mobile Ads (AdMob), and Google UMP for sign-in, cloud saves, requested AI hints, advertising, and privacy choices. These services process relevant information under their own terms and privacy disclosures. We do not sell your personal information. We may disclose information where legally required or necessary to protect users, the service, or security.

Retention and deletion

Guest data remains on your device until you clear app storage or uninstall the app. Cloud account and game data remain in Firebase while the account is active and are removed when you delete your BlockPulse account. Provider-held backup copies may be retained temporarily under the provider's retention and deletion processes or as required by law.

To delete your BlockPulse account and associated cloud profile, campaign progress, and leaderboard entry, open Settings in the app and choose Delete account. If you cannot access the app, use the BlockPulse account deletion page or email syokusoglu1@gmail.com from the Google account address you used to sign in. Do not send your password. Deleting your BlockPulse account does not delete your Google account.

Security and international processing

Connections to Google services use encrypted network transport. Firebase Authentication and Firestore access controls are used to protect cloud data. Google services may process information in countries outside your country of residence under their applicable privacy terms. No method of internet transmission or storage can be guaranteed completely secure.

Children

BlockPulse is intended for a general audience and is not specifically directed to children. We do not knowingly seek to collect personal information from children. Parents or guardians who have questions or believe a child has provided information can contact us at the email below.

Your choices and privacy rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information, object to certain processing, or withdraw consent where processing is based on consent. Contact us at syokusoglu1@gmail.com. You can also manage ad choices in the app's UMP privacy options when available and in your device's Google advertising settings.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy as the app or its data practices change. We will post the current version here and update the effective date.
